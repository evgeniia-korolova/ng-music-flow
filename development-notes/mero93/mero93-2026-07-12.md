**What I did in this week**

This week was more for consolidation and testing application. Teammate found out that sometimes playlist creating endpoint was failing. This was happening because of mandatory requirement of at least 1 valid track to be present. It turned out it was not an issue with playlist creating, but how jamendo handles requests. As it turns out, jamendo will throttle some(or most) of the read requests too. So Jamendo would silently fail to retrieve a track with a specific ID, playlist would have been empty and that would cause a failure. To fix this, I added a request repeat logic with 0.5 seconds delay in-between. As I tested, this seems to have fixed an issue. But then again, it might fail when loading playlist with lots of Jamendo songs. This is unfortunate, but not our fault. Just a limitation of free tier Jamendo account.
